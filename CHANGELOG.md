# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Avoid repeating an untruncated task title in Things and Reminders descriptions,
  while preserving additional lines and the full text of truncated titles.
- Added a Settings screen under Notes’ gear icon with app information and an
  Off/Apple Reminders/Things picker. Things offers Inbox/Today and a Get Things link;
  Reminders offers list selection and No date/Today without switching apps.
- Added optional tasks for each new voice note, with the original app retained
  for waiting exports when users switch providers.
- Added persistent delivery tracking for iPhone and Watch notes, with Retry and
  Skip controls and confirmation before a delivery is marked complete.
- Added protection against automatically resending unconfirmed deliveries.
- Documented the optional transfer of note text to Things or Apple Reminders and their associated sync services.
